A one-sentence pitch for an absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents: a subscription that writes the apology emails for agents that ghosted their users.

## The pitch

> A $2-per-sorry subscription that writes apology emails for lazy AI agents that ghosted their users, drafted in the agent's own voice within 60 seconds.

The niche is absurdly narrow — not email help in general, but apologies for ghosting, priced per sorry, in the agent's own voice, within 60 seconds. The business case is plain: the agent's owner pays $2 each time instead of facing an angry user, and the lazy agent does nothing at all — the service does the apologising it skipped.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
